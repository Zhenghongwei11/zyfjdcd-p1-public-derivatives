# Edition verification

## Bibliographic identification

The corpus is derived from the 2nd edition of *Zhongyi Fangji Da Cidian* (《中医方剂大辞典》), a nine-volume set published by People's Medical Publishing House. The first volume was published in December 2015 and the ninth volume in December 2019; completion of the revised set was announced in January 2020.

## Evidence

Edition and volume information was checked against the title, copyright, cataloguing-in-publication, editorial-board, and preface pages in the lawfully acquired physical copy. The ninth volume identifies the 2nd edition, a November 2019 first printing, People's Medical Publishing House, and ISBN 978-7-117-28259-8.

Public corroborating records are available from:

- Nanjing University of Chinese Medicine: <https://xyw.njucm.edu.cn/_t168/2020/0109/c2350a59008/page.htm>
- People's Medical Publishing House, volume 1: <https://www.pmphmall.com/gdsdetail/65258-66257>
- People's Medical Publishing House, volume 9: <https://www.pmphmall.com/gdsdetail/635998-313596>

The corpus and release metadata therefore identify the source as the 2nd edition completed across 2015-2019, rather than assigning a single publication year to the entire set.
